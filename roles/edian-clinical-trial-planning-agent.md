# Product Engineer - Clinical Trial Planning Agent

**Company:** [Edian](../companies/edian.md)

**Type:** Engineering — 2-6 weeks, 20-40 hrs/week

**Rate:** To agree

**Location:** London-based; remote work is fine

## The opportunity

Planning a clinical trial takes months of specialist work: feasibility, regulation, costing, operations. All of it before a single patient is enrolled.

Hundreds of thousands of studies are registered worldwide, and tens of thousands more are registered every year. Today that planning work can mean a small expert team, a mountain of documents, and months of assembly by hand.

## What we're building

We are building the Clinical Trial Planning Agent to change the scale of that work. Imagine having a thousand brilliant researchers working together on every trial plan: the reading, the cross-checking and the drafting - assumptions, feasibility, timeline, budget and the study documents - so human experts spend their time on judgement, not assembly.

The ambition is superintelligent planning. Plans in days, not months. Every relevant data point considered, not just the ones someone had time to find. Figures that stay consistent across every document, every number traceable to its source. The calculations run in deterministic code, not guesses. The agent drafts; a person reviews and signs off.

If we get this right, planning could be dramatically better: trials starting sooner, more treatments tested, fewer stalling on paperwork. For patients, that could mean earlier access to medicines that might help them - and fewer that never arrive.

That is the point of all of it.

The foundation exists. Now we need the engineer who turns it into the tool the industry plans every trial with.

## The role

You will build it end to end: a simple intake and planning thread, review tools for planning and costing teams, document and data workflows, and agent actions that stay dependable in regulated workflows.

The stack is Next.js, React, TypeScript, Tailwind and Supabase, with Anthropic integrations and Vitest/Playwright tests. We want strong full-stack delivery, care for useful interfaces, and experience making AI features reliable where mistakes are expensive.

## The details

Location: London-based; remote work is fine. Project length 2-6 weeks at 20-40 hours/week. Rate: to agree.
